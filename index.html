About Me

Hey, I'm Sanjar.

Welcome! This is my personal space where I write posts about myself, my life, and random thoughts built right from my point of view.

I'm from Khanka, Khorezm, Uzbekistan, a place I've called home my entire life. I currently study at Khanka Specialized School, where I'm constantly exploring new ideas and looking for opportunities to learn beyond the classroom.

I'm passionate about football, movies, and coding—especially what I like to call vibe coding, where creativity matters just as much as the code itself. I enjoy building projects, experimenting with new technologies, and turning ideas into something people can actually use.

Outside of programming, you'll usually find me watching a great film, following football, or scrolling through social media that helps me to get refreshed. I'm especially interested in fantasy, science fiction, and stories that leave a lasting impression.

I'm someone who believes in continuous growth. Whether it's improving my English, learning new technical skills, or creating personal projects, I enjoy the process of becoming a little better every day.

This website is my digital corner—a place where I share what I'm building, what I'm learning, and the journey I'm on.

Feel free to look around, read my blog posts, or reach out directly using the automated forms below!
