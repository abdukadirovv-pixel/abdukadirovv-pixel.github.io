Interstellar

PARAMOUNT PICTURES • RATING: 9.5/10
FAVORITE CHARACTER: Joseph Cooper

The absolute peak of modern sci-fi cinema. Christopher Nolan really created a masterpice by visualizing the vastness of space and the emotional depth of human connection. I really got trumatized until I watched it more than two times. After the first time, I was not able to find answers of some questions such as "where did he get stuck?" or "why he told her daughter that he is coming back, even if he knew he won't?" So yeah I highly recommend to give it a try.
